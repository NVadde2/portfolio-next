Today marks the 50th anniversary of the **[Rubik's Cube](https://www.rubiks.com/history)**. Invented in 1974 by Hungarian architect Erno Rubik, the cube has become one of the most beloved toys of all time, inspiring inquiring minds and puzzlers across the globe. Over the past five decades, millions have been captivated by its challenging twists and turns, trying to unlock the puzzle's seemingly endless possibilities (well, 43 quintillion to be exact!). Whether you're a seasoned speedcuber or just getting started, the Rubik's Cube continues to offer something for everyone.

When I was a kid, I had a Rubik's Cube. For years, I would occasionally pick it up and try to solve it, always attempting to complete one side at a time. Despite my efforts, I never got past that stage. I could complete one side but never quite solved the entire puzzle. While I never quite mastered the cube as a child, my love for it never waned.

But last year, something changed. My 7-year-old cousin got his hands on a Rubik's Cube, and for him, it was love at first twist. He was determined to solve it. I knew how much he wanted to crack it because every morning, I'd hear the sound of the cube twisting from his room. I could also tell when he had fallen asleep at night because the twisting would finally stop.

His determination to solve the puzzle reignited my own interest, and together, we embarked on a journey to finally crack it. It didn't take long for me to realize that I had been approaching the cube completely wrong. Instead of trying to solve one side at a time, you need to work on solving it one layer at a time—top, middle, and bottom. After figuring that out, I taught him the algorithms to solve the cube.

Within a couple of weeks, he had it mastered. Now, he "cubes" every day for hours at a time and has been doing so for months. When I watch him with the cube, it's like he's in the zone—completely focused and absorbed in the moment, lost to time and space. No matter how much I scramble the cube, he usually solves it in under 30 seconds, often multiple times in a row. I truly believe in him, and I think he might even break a world record someday. I've never seen him this focused or driven by anything before. His excitement was contagious, and soon I was introducing him to new challenges.

![Solving the cube](/images/hobbies/rubiks-solve.gif)

Solving the same Rubik's Cube over and over can lose its spark, so we've been exploring different varieties to keep the challenge fresh. In fact, there are 45 known types of cubes that can make your cubing experience more interesting. We've already tried a few, like the Mirror Cube, 4x4 Cube, Snake Cube, Speed Cube, and the Megaminx. Each one brings its own unique twists—*literally!*

The next one on our list is the **Axis Cube**, which I'm excited to dive into. So on this special day, I'm reminded that exploring these different puzzles has been both fun and challenging, but more importantly, it's shown me the power of curiosity and perseverance. I hope we, puzzlers continue to discover the joy of solving, learning, and persevering through the endless possibilities hidden within these twisting layers.

If you're stuck on your cube or just need a new challenge, why not join our little cubing duo? We can crack puzzles together, have some fun, and maybe even find your next favorite cube. Trust me, it's more fun with company—let's twist and solve!
